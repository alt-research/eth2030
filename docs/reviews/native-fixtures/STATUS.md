# Native signed Osaka and Amsterdam fixture work in progress

The strict native runner consumes signed EEST bytes, preserves complete envelope fields, validates input/selector/root/log metadata, and fails missing/unsupported/unknown cases. Osaka and Amsterdam are registered explicitly, with official `slotNumber` and coherent inherited BPO schedules. Five official historical envelope fixtures are always exercised by default tests; five independently executed Amsterdam envelope fixtures and three invalid blob envelopes reach the actual native parser/transition. The expanded SDK corpus matches 50/50 Amsterdam cases; six intentionally direct Osaka over-cap diagnostics remain separate outer-consumer work.

All 1,223 selected historical EEST v5.4.0 Osaka cases passed after composing the separate authorizations, ModExp, finalization and CLZ corrections. The newer official EELS tests@v21.0.2 corpus has 14,665 Osaka and 16,908 Amsterdam cases. Adapter refinements and real VM/validation corrections are still being qualified against that complete set. Every mismatch and unknown exception remains a failure. Complete CI wiring, final corpus evidence and dedicated PR publication remain pending.
